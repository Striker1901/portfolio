Objective

To design Orbikun's own typeface — a sans that carries the studio's voice across the website, social media and identity, and replaces licensed fonts with something the studio owns.

Final Proposal

Smooth is a geometric-leaning sans with soft, continuous curves and open counters, built in Glyphs as a single Regular with 286 glyphs. The launch film shows the letters being drawn — anchors, Bézier handles, weight — before they settle into words, cut to the beat at 60 fps.
