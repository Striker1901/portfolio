Objective

PadelGOD runs a weekly padel league and a YouTube channel that follows it. The brief: a new identity and symbol, a redesigned website, and a system that stretches from the court to merch and events — caps, wristbands, towels, team kit, trophies and sponsor boards.

Final Proposal

Movement is the core of the concept: capturing the intervals of the ball. The symbol is a padel ball cut into slices, so it already draws motion by itself. Court blue and a sharp lime carry the whole system, set in Anybody ExtraBold — the world can move and blur around it, the mark always stays sharp.
